Construction & demolition debris, handled.

Remodels, demos, and tear-outs are what our bins are built for. Drywall, framing lumber, flooring, cabinets, and tile are all covered by our flat rate — no special "construction" pricing. Here's what to expect.

What goes in

  • Drywall & plaster
  • Framing lumber & studs
  • Flooring, carpet & padding
  • Cabinets, countertops & vanities
  • Tile, cement board & backsplash
  • Doors, trim & fixtures
  • Insulation, siding & stucco

What it usually weighs

Bathroom demo: about 1 to 2 tons. Tile, plaster, and a cast iron tub push it toward 2.

Kitchen remodel: about 2 to 3 tons in a 20 yard.

Whole-house gut or framing job: 3 tons or more per bin — plan on swapping bins as they fill.

Every bin includes weight: 1 ton on the 15 yard, 2 tons on the 20 yard. Over that is $165 per ton.

Recycling receipts for permits

Construction loads go to certified construction & demolition recycling facilities, not straight to the landfill. If your project has a City of San Diego or County building permit, ask us for the scale ticket — you'll need it to get your C&D deposit refunded.

Concrete, dirt, brick, asphalt, and roofing tear-offs are heavy and priced separately — call us for a quote. Not sure which size? See dumpster sizes or give us a call.

Frequently Asked Questions
What's included in my dumpster rental?+

Delivery, pickup, and disposal are all included, along with 3 full days and 1 ton of weight on the 15 yard or 2 tons on the 20 yard. Extra weight is $165 per ton and extra days are $25 each. The price we quote is the price you pay — no hidden fees.

What is typically included in a dumpster rental service?+

A dumpster rental service generally includes the delivery of the dumpster to your specified location, a set rental period for you to fill the dumpster, and the subsequent pickup and disposal of the waste.

How is the dumpster delivered to my residence?+

Dumpsters are delivered by specialized trucks that can safely place the dumpster in the desired location at your residence, typically a driveway or another designated spot.

How long can I keep the dumpster?+

Every rental includes 3 full days. Need it longer? Keep it for $25 a day — just let us know and we'll work with your schedule. Need it for more than a week? Call us for a weekly rate.

If I need to keep the dumpster longer than expected, how will that affect my final bill?+

If you require the dumpster for additional time beyond the originally planned rental period, there are two scenarios. If the dumpster needs to be emptied and then returned for further use, this will be treated as a new rental with corresponding charges.

How far in advance do I need to book?+

We can often deliver next-day, and sometimes same-day. Booking a few days ahead helps guarantee your preferred delivery date, especially for weekends.

How do I prepare for delivery?+

Clear a flat, accessible space about the size of two parking spots — your driveway or the street both work. Just make sure there's nothing overhead, like low branches or wires.

Is renting multiple dumpsters at the same time an option?+

Absolutely, we can facilitate the rental of more than one dumpster to support your project, and we offer advice to help you identify the best approach for waste removal.

What can't go in the dumpster?+

We can't accept hazardous materials like paint, oils, solvents, gasoline, chemicals, propane tanks, fluorescent bulbs, asbestos, tires, or batteries. Not sure about something? Just ask us.

Can drywall go in a dumpster?+

Yes. Drywall, plaster, framing lumber, flooring, cabinets, and tile all go in our bins and are covered by the flat rate. Just keep in mind that drywall is heavy — a gutted room adds up fast — so a 20 yard with 2 tons included is the safer bet for a full remodel.

What size dumpster do I need for a bathroom remodel in San Diego?+

A 15 yard dumpster handles most bathroom remodels. The debris usually weighs 1 to 2 tons, so if you're pulling out tile, plaster, or a cast iron tub, go with the 15 yard or step up to the 20 yard for the extra ton included.

What size dumpster do I need for a kitchen remodel?+

A 20 yard dumpster is the right fit for most kitchen remodels. Cabinets, countertops, flooring, and drywall from a full kitchen typically run 2 to 3 tons, and the 20 yard includes 2 tons.

Does construction debris cost more than household junk?+

No. Remodel and demolition debris — drywall, lumber, flooring, cabinets, tile — is priced the same as household junk: the flat rate with weight included. Only dense materials like concrete, dirt, brick, asphalt, and roofing tear-offs are quoted separately, because they weigh far more per load.

Can I get a recycling receipt for my building permit?+

Yes. Construction loads go to certified construction and demolition recycling facilities, and we can provide the scale ticket. The City of San Diego and the County require receipts from a certified facility to refund your C&D deposit, so let us know your job has a permit when you book.
