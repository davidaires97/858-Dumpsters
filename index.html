What can go in the dumpster?

We accept most construction debris, household junk, yard waste, and renovation materials. Not sure about something? Just ask.

✓ Accepted Materials

  • Construction debris
  • Household junk
  • Furniture
  • Drywall, lumber & flooring
  • Roofing shingles
  • Yard waste & branches
  • Cabinets & countertops
  • Carpet & padding
  • Cardboard, packaging & scrap metal

✕ Not Accepted

  • Hazardous materials
  • Paint
  • Oils
  • Solvents
  • Asbestos
  • Tires
  • Batteries
  • Gasoline
  • Chemicals
  • Propane tanks
  • Fluorescent bulbs
