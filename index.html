Frequently Asked Questions
What's included in my dumpster rental?+

Delivery, pickup, and disposal are all included, along with 1 ton of weight and up to 5 days of rental time. The price we quote is the price you pay — no hidden fees.

How long can I keep the dumpster?+

Every rental includes up to 5 days. Need more time to finish your project? Just give us a call — we're happy to work with your schedule.

How far in advance do I need to book?+

We can often deliver next-day, and sometimes same-day. Booking a few days ahead helps guarantee your preferred delivery date, especially for weekends.

How do I prepare for delivery?+

Clear a flat, accessible space about the size of two parking spots — your driveway or the street both work. Just make sure there's nothing overhead, like low branches or wires.

What can't go in the dumpster?+

We can't accept hazardous materials like paint, oils, solvents, gasoline, chemicals, propane tanks, fluorescent bulbs, asbestos, tires, or batteries. Not sure about something? Just ask us.
